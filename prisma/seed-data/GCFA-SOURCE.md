# GCFA statistical source data

Río Texas (tenant #1) church statistics ultimately derive from GCFA local-church statistical
tables. The full 25-year (2000–2024) source — all tables, plus predecessor conferences
Rio Grande (#531) and Southwest Texas (#592) — is housed canonically in **`church-documents`**:

- Parsed CSVs + data dictionary: `documents/conferences/rio-texas/gcfa/`
  https://github.com/wilsonpruitt/church-documents/tree/main/documents/conferences/rio-texas/gcfa
- Original workbook (GCFA Service Ticket #726954):
  `documents/conferences/rio-texas/source/gcfa-statistics-2000-2024.xlsx`
- Manifest ids: `rio-texas-gcfa-statistics` · `src-rio-texas-gcfa-statistics-2000-2024`

For additional conferences as Field Guide scales, look for their GCFA data under the
corresponding `documents/conferences/<conf>/` path in church-documents.
